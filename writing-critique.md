# Writing Critique Example

**Original text:**
"Remote work has become very popular and companies are using it more because it helps them save money and workers like it so they stay longer at the company which is good for everyone involved in the long term."

**Improved version:**
"Remote work has become more common as companies seek to reduce office costs and retain employees. Its flexibility may also make it more attractive to some workers."

## Key Improvements

* **Improved sentence structure** — Replaced a long chain of clauses with two concise sentences.
* **Removed vague language** — Replaced phrases such as "very popular" and "good for everyone" with more specific wording.
* **Improved clarity** — Separated the ideas of cost savings, employee retention, and employee preferences.
* **Improved logical precision** — Changed the absolute implication that workers will stay longer because they like remote work to a more cautious statement about its potential appeal.
* **Improved readability** — Reduced repetition and made the relationships between ideas easier to follow.

## Critique

The original sentence contains several related ideas but connects them with repeated uses of "and" and "so," making the logical relationships difficult to follow. It also uses vague expressions such as "very popular" and "good for everyone involved."

The revision preserves the original meaning while making the claims more precise and separating distinct ideas into manageable sentences. The use of "may" also avoids presenting employee retention or preference as a guaranteed outcome.
