# Writing Critique Example

## Original Text

> Remote work has become very popular and companies are using it more because it helps them save money and workers like it so they stay longer at the company which is good for everyone involved in the long term.

## Improved Version

> Remote work has become more common. Companies may adopt it to reduce office costs and improve employee retention, since many workers value greater flexibility.

## Key Improvements

- Broke an overloaded sentence into two sentences.
- Replaced vague wording such as "very popular" and "good for everyone."
- Made the cause-and-effect relationships more explicit.
- Replaced an absolute claim about retention with qualified language.
- Reduced repetition and improved readability.
