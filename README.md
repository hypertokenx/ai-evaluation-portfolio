# AI Evaluation Portfolio

This repository contains samples of structured AI response evaluation and clear analytical writing.

The samples focus on skills relevant to generalist AI evaluation work:

* Identifying factual errors and unsupported claims
* Assessing reasoning quality, completeness, and consistency
* Providing clear, evidence-based written feedback
* Rewriting responses for improved accuracy and clarity

## Evaluation Approach

Evaluations consider factors such as factual accuracy, relevance, reasoning quality, completeness, clarity, and adherence to the task.

## Contents

* `ai-response-evaluation.md` — Structured evaluation of an AI-generated answer, including errors, reasoning analysis, and a revised response
* `writing-critique.md` — Before/after clarity improvement with explanation
* `complex-topic-explained.md` — Clear, structured explanation of a complex idea
* `reasoning-analysis.md` — Breakdown of logical strengths and weaknesses

All examples prioritize clarity, precision, evidence-based judgment, and structured reasoning.
